===== slide12.xml =====
Functional Test: Everything pertains the actual UUT testing needs
It should describe system or system element functions or tasks to desired system performance under stated conditions. 
They include customer desires, operational constraints, regulatory restrictions, and implementation realities, examples:  
Operational requirements describe how the operator interfaces with the test solution system, and support teams, and related functions or equipment (Consider normal and abnormal conditions).
Performance, a characteristic of function, requirements describe function or system attributes useful to the test solution operation such as expected performance, accuracy, fidelity, range, resolution, speed, and response times (Performance is normally expressed quantitatively).
System Modes and States Requirements defines the various operational modes or states.
Interface requirements are the system’s physical inputs and outputs, and a description of how they operate/behave with other systems and human elements.

===== slide3.xml =====
Environmental, Health and Safety Requirements:
Requirements directed to human factors relate to human users and their interaction with the system such as efficiency, health effects, and communication. Related terms are Human Engineering and Human System Integration. Industry standards to consider include MIL-STD-882, MIL-STD-1472, MIL-STD-46855, and SAE SAE6906.[GC1] [EKC2] 
A dedicated Safety Engineer or a designated SE is responsible to determine, in coordination with other disciplines and agencies, the safety requirements for a test solution. 
Requirements defined to prevent failure conditions or to provide safety related functions are uniquely identified and traceable.
